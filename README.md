The Silent Stacks — a top-down stealth game. You are a thief in the grand archive after dark: slip past the patrolling wardens and their lantern vision cones, hide behind the bookshelves, take every ledger, and reach the green door unseen. Three floors, each with more wardens and tighter shelves. Run loud and the wardens hear your feet; sneak, wait for the cone to turn, or throw a coin to pull a warden away. If a warden's gaze fills, the alarm rises and the chase begins — break line of sight to slip away again.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD or arrows move, Shift sneaks, F throws a coin, P pauses, M mutes.
